Made for families

  • Works on kids' Apple Watch with Family Setup — no iPhone of their own needed.
  • Parents decide who a child can talk to. New contacts and groups need a guardian's approval, and guardians set the hours when messages may play.
  • Talk one-to-one or in groups: the whole family, just the kids, grandparents, or friends from another family.
  • Invite people via Messages, with a QR code, or by reading out a short code. Adults can leave or switch family at any time.

Live when it matters

  • On a supported watch a message plays by itself as soon as it arrives — no tap, even with the wrist down.
  • After each message Wristalk stays live for 10 minutes, so replies come through instantly. Then it goes quiet again and saves battery.
  • Want it live all day? Turn on Keep Wristalk live, with auto-off after 1, 2 or 4 hours or tonight, and a battery guard.
  • Nothing plays during Do Not Disturb or Sleep — you see what you missed. Stop a message while it plays, and delete missed ones.

Private by design

  • Every voice message is end-to-end encrypted. Only the people in the conversation can hear it — not us, not anyone else.
  • Messages are deleted once they have been played, or after 10 minutes at the latest. If someone missed you, you're told.
  • Report or block anyone who bothers you; guardians can do it for their children.
  • Sign in with Apple. No email address, no phone number, no ads, no tracking.

On iPhone, too

  • The iPhone app is the comfortable place to manage people, groups and kids' settings, and to approve requests.
  • It is a walkie-talkie of its own, with Apple's system Push to Talk — messages play even while Wristalk is closed.
  • So parents without an Apple Watch can talk with the family too.

Which Apple Watch?

Live through the speaker

Apple Watch SE (3rd generation), Series 10 and later, Ultra 2 and later. Messages play by themselves through the speaker.

Notifications or headphones

Other watches with watchOS 26, such as Apple Watch SE (2nd generation) and Series 6 to 8. The full app works; messages play live with Bluetooth headphones, otherwise you tap a notification to listen.

Not supported

Watches that can't run watchOS 26.

Apple Watch: watchOS 26 or later. iPhone: iOS 26 or later. Each watch or iPhone needs an internet connection (Wi-Fi or cellular). Available in the US App Store.

One Family Pass for everyone

Family Pass

$19.99/year

First week free

Free to download. US price; the App Store shows it before you confirm.

  • One person subscribes. Apple Family Sharing shares the Family Pass with your Apple family — kids' watches included.
  • Each family needs its own Family Pass, including relatives and friends you talk with who are in another family (see the Terms).
  • Renews automatically every year. Cancel at least 24 hours before renewal in your Apple Account settings (Settings → your name → Subscriptions).
  • New iPhone or watch? Tap Restore purchases on the Family Pass screen.
  • If it ends, Wristalk shows the Family Pass screen and your family can't talk until someone subscribes again. Your family, contacts and groups stay.
